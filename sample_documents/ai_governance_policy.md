# Internal AI Governance Policy (Excerpt)

## Approved Use of Third-Party Models
Any use of externally hosted large language models on customer PII must go
through the Data Protection Office for a Data Processing Impact Assessment
(DPIA) before deployment. Customer financial data may not leave approved
regional data boundaries.

## Human-in-the-Loop Requirement
Any AI system that materially influences a credit decision must retain a
human reviewer with override authority. Fully autonomous credit decisioning
is not currently permitted under internal policy.

## Model Risk Management
All production ML/AI models must be registered in the Model Risk Inventory,
undergo independent validation, and be reviewed annually. Generative AI
systems used in customer-facing communication require a documented
hallucination/quality monitoring plan before go-live.

## Data Retention
Any document or transcript processed by an AI system that contains personal
data must follow the standard 7-year retention schedule and must be
deletable on request in line with data subject rights procedures.
